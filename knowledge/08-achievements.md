Zubia was runner-up at GameCon, the game development competition at NED University of Engineering and Technology.

In 2025 she took part in a game jam, building a multiplayer game with a team within the jam's time limit. The Masked Regime, a two-player local co-op obstacle race on the theme of masks, is one of her game-jam games.

She also took part in the Game Development Hackathon at IBA University, Karachi, building a game as part of a team.
