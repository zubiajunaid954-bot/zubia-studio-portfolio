The Unbroken Oath is a story-driven first-person tactical shooter and rescue mission with stealth-style enemy awareness, built in Unity 2022.3 (built-in render pipeline) for Windows. It was developed by a team of four called Dazzling Devs: Samrah, Duaa, Zubia and Maha. The heroine is named Samrah.

The game is set in a ruined Sun Temple. Four friends are separated when night falls, and Samrah, the only one left, must free the other three. The player fights smart enemies with a rifle, a sniper rifle and a pistol, and uses grenades, medkits and a live minimap. An AI operator guides the player through day, night and storm conditions. A trailer is available on its card in the Project Gallery of this site.
