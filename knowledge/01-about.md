Zubia Junaid is a game developer focused on Unity, Unreal Engine and VR/XR. She is a final-year Computer Science and IT (CSIT) student at NED University of Engineering and Technology in Karachi, Pakistan, specialising in Gaming and Animation (2023 to 2027).

Her main focus is gameplay programming in C# with Unity: game logic, win and lose conditions, level design, enemy AI (patrols, sight and noise detection, NavMesh), character animation systems and UI. She builds VR experiences for Meta Quest with the XR Interaction Toolkit (XR Origin, hand and controller interaction, teleportation, grabbing, socket puzzles and spatial audio). She also builds endless runners in Unreal Engine with Blueprints, and interactive 3D for the web with Three.js.

She has completed three game-studio internships (Game Storm Studios, Treeobit and TechCami) and freelance work, and some of her games are free to play on itch.io.
