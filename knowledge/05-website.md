This website is Zubia's interactive 3D portfolio, built as a semester project with React, Vite, Three.js (React Three Fiber) and Blender. It is presented as a miniature 3D game studio. When the site opens, her avatar walks into the lobby and waves, and as you scroll the camera glides room to room: Lobby, About Room, Skills Station, Career Path, Project Gallery, Trophy Wall, Screening Room and Contact Desk.

Her avatar is her own character: generated as a base mesh with Meshy AI from a reference image, then optimised, rigged with a 19-bone skeleton, skin-weighted and animated in Blender 5 with seven animations (idle, walk, wave, point, guide, present, celebrate). The avatar's head follows the mouse, she waves when clicked, and her hoodie takes the colour of the active theme.

The site has four colour themes (Amber Night, Violet, Lime and Forest, Mono Light), a simple 2D view for devices that cannot run 3D, a section bar to jump to any area, a projects gallery with filters and search, a downloadable CV, and a 48-second promo video called "Behind the Doors", made in Adobe After Effects, that plays in the Screening Room.

This AI assistant answers questions about Zubia using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over her CV and project notes, with Google Gemini.
